Privacy Policy

How we collect and use your information when you use this site.

Information Collected

We collect information you submit through forms, including name, email, topic, and message content.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, improve trip planning support, and maintain service quality.

Data Storage

Saved itineraries are stored locally in your browser. Contact messages and community submissions are stored privately on Vercel. When email notifications are configured, Resend delivers the message to the site owner. Trip-planning inputs are sent to our AI provider to produce a draft.

Sharing and questions

We do not sell personal data. Service providers process information needed to operate this site, including Vercel for hosting and private message storage, Resend for email notifications, and OpenAI for itinerary generation. To ask about stored contact messages or request their removal, contact the site owner.
