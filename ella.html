Ella

Take the slow route through tea country

Editorial review: 17 November 2026 · Suggested durations and journey times are planning estimates, not live schedules.

Suggested stay2–3 nights
Route ideaKandy → Ella → south coast

Make the most of your visit

Choose one main walk per day and leave room for changing mountain weather. Combine viewpoints with a relaxed town meal, and avoid walking on railway tracks.

  • Plan a morning walk with a local guide
  • Explore tea country at a relaxed pace
  • Use designated viewpoints around the railway

Getting there and getting around

Treat the Kandy–Ella rail journey as a full travel day. Check the official timetable and reserved-seat availability; a scenic route is not a guarantee of an available seat.

What to budget for

Train fares depend on service and class. Check Sri Lanka Railways for a quote, and ask separately about local transfers and any guided walks.

Use the budget guide for illustrative daily ranges. Get a dated quote for your actual travel dates.

Accessibility and practical considerations

Hills, steep paths and uneven surfaces are common. Ask accommodation providers about vehicle access, steps and room location; trails are not assumed to be step-free.
