Central Province

Kandy

A cultural stop between lowlands and hills

Editorial review: November 2026 · Suggested durations and journey times are planning estimates, not live schedules.

Suggested stay2 nights
Route ideaSigiriya → Kandy → Ella

Make the most of your visit

Give the Temple of the Tooth and the lakeside area their own day. Keep another half-day flexible for gardens or a slower city visit. Respect worshippers and follow on-site dress and photography instructions.

  • Visit the Temple of the Tooth respectfully
  • Take time around the lake
  • Continue into hill country after a restful night

Getting there and getting around

Kandy is connected to Colombo by road and rail. Check rail schedules and leave a buffer for city traffic; continue towards Ella on a separate travel day.

What to budget for

Confirm temple admission and any guide charges directly. Rail tickets, taxis and cultural performances are separate expenses; do not rely on an old quoted fee.

Use the budget guide for illustrative daily ranges. Get a dated quote for your actual travel dates.

Accessibility and practical considerations

The city has slopes and busy roads. Ask the temple and accommodation providers about accessible entrances, steps and assistance before arrival.
