A good place to start

Less rushing. More Sri Lanka.

Three suggested routes with room for travel days and unexpected discoveries. Adapt them to your arrival time, season and mobility needs.

Editorial review: 17 November 2026. These are planning suggestions, not bookable packages. Check transport schedules before reserving accommodation.

7 days · Suggested route

A first taste

Colombo / Negombo → Sigiriya → Kandy → Colombo

  1. Day 1

    Arrive and rest near Colombo or Negombo.

  2. Days 2–3

    Travel to Sigiriya, then leave a full day for the fortress and gardens.

  3. Days 4–5

    Transfer to Kandy and spend a full day exploring at a relaxed pace.

  4. Days 6–7

    Return towards Colombo, with a buffer before your flight.

10 days · Suggested route

Culture into tea country

Colombo → Sigiriya → Kandy → Ella → Colombo

  1. Day 1

    Arrival and a quiet first night.

  2. Days 2–3

    Explore Sigiriya and the surrounding area.

  3. Days 4–5

    Travel to Kandy and make time for its cultural sights.

  4. Days 6–8

    Set aside a travel day for the hills, then enjoy two days in Ella.

  5. Days 9–10

    Return to Colombo with an overnight buffer before departure.

    6. Customize this idea

14 days · Suggested route

Hills, wildlife and coast

Colombo → Sigiriya → Kandy → Ella → Yala area → Galle → Colombo

  1. Day 1

    Rest after arrival.

  2. Days 2–3

    Sigiriya and nearby heritage sites.

  3. Days 4–5

    Kandy, without rushing the transfer day.

  4. Days 6–8

    Journey into tea country and settle into Ella.

  5. Days 9–10

    Transfer to the Yala area and take a responsibly operated safari.

  6. Days 11–12

    Travel to Galle and slow down by the coast.

  7. Days 13–14

    Return to Colombo and allow a departure buffer.

    8. Customize this idea

Before you choose

Long transfers can take most of a day. Keep your final night close to the departure airport and check seasonal conditions before committing to a coast or park.
