Yala National Park

Make space for wildlife, without rushing it

Editorial review: November 2026 · Suggested durations and journey times are planning estimates, not live schedules.

Suggested stay1–2 nights
Route ideaElla → Yala area → south coast

Make the most of your visit

Allow an overnight stay before an early park visit. Choose an operator who follows park rules, keeps distance from animals and does not promise a particular sighting.

  • Choose a responsible safari operator
  • Leave room for rest after the drive
  • Observe wildlife without feeding or approaching animals

Getting there and getting around

Arrange a road transfer to accommodation near your selected park entrance. Confirm the meeting point with the operator; different entrances can add substantial driving time.

What to budget for

Ask for an itemized quote covering park admission, vehicle, guide, taxes and pickup. Confirm opening status and permitted areas with the Department of Wildlife Conservation.

Use the budget guide for illustrative daily ranges. Get a dated quote for your actual travel dates.

Accessibility and practical considerations

Safari vehicles involve climbing into a seat and travelling on uneven tracks. Discuss transfer assistance, seating, ride length and toilet stops with the operator before booking.
